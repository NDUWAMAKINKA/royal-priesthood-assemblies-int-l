Privacy

Your privacy matters to us.

How Royal Priesthood Assemblies International handles personal information shared through this website.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Royal Priesthood Assemblies International is a church based in Lusaka, Zambia. You can reach us at royalpriesthoodassembliesintl@gmail.com or on +260 977 490 080.

What we collect

When you use a form on this website, you may choose to share your name, email address, phone number, prayer request, testimony, visit details or other information you include in your message.

Our hosting provider may also process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited, in order to serve the site securely.

How we use it

  • To respond to your prayer request, enquiry or message.
  • To help you plan a visit, arrange transport or find a home cell.
  • To follow up on a testimony or connect card, with your consent.
  • To keep the website secure and understand how it is used.

Email forms

Some forms on this site open your own email application so that you can send a message directly to the church. In that case, your email is handled by your email provider and by the church’s email service.

The video conference registration form sends your email address to the church’s booking service for the purpose of sending you the meeting link. It is not added to a marketing list.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website, email or video meetings, or where we are required to do so by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent for future contact. Email royalpriesthoodassembliesintl@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we can.

Children

We care about the privacy of children. Please do not submit personal information about a child through this website without the consent of a parent or guardian. Children’s ministry records are handled separately by the church office.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information shared with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email royalpriesthoodassembliesintl@gmail.com or write to us at Otwa Events, Long Acres, Ndeke Road, Lusaka, Zambia.
