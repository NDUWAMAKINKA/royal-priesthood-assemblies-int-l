Who we are

A church built on prayer and fellowship

A Christian church in Lusaka, Zambia, growing together through worship, the Word, and service to our neighbours.

Rooted in faith. United in fellowship.

Royal Priesthood Assemblies International is a Christian church based in Lusaka, Zambia. Our church life is centred on worship, biblical teaching, prayer, fellowship, and service.

We seek to create a welcoming community where people can learn Scripture, strengthen their faith, and serve others with compassion.

Explore our ministries

Our Founder

Apostle Dr. Mighty Zunga

General Overseer and Founder

Apostle Dr. Mighty Zunga is the Founder and General Overseer of Royal Priesthood Assemblies International in Lusaka, Zambia. His ministry is expressed through preaching, prayer, discipleship, and the leadership of the church community. He encourages believers to grow in their understanding of Scripture and to put their faith into practice through service.
