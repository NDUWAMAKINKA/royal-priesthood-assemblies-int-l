Our story

A story of love, faith and family.

Royal Priesthood Assemblies International began with a call to gather God’s people in Lusaka — to teach His Word, make room for His presence, and love one another well.

Meet our shepherdOur ministries

The beginning

It started with one instruction.

Gather my people. Teach them my Word. Make room for my presence.

That conviction became a church family. What began quietly — in prayer, conversation and a handful of believing hearts — grew into a house where children, youth, parents and grandparents could worship together.

We did not begin with a building. We began with a people. And that is still who we are: a family learning to love God and one another in everyday life.

Our confession

“But you are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, His own special people, that you may proclaim the praises of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light.”

1 Peter 2:9

Our founder

A life given to the Word and to people.

Apostle Dr. Mighty Zunga is the Founder and General Overseer of Royal Priesthood Assemblies International in Lusaka, Zambia.

His ministry is expressed through preaching, prayer, discipleship and the leadership of the church community. He encourages believers to grow in their understanding of Scripture and to put their faith into practice through service.

Under his leadership, the church has become a family where the Word is taught with clarity, prayer is given room, and every person is encouraged to take their place.

What we are building

Rooted in faith. United in fellowship.

Our mission, vision and values are simple enough to remember and strong enough to build a life on.

Our mission

Faith in word and action.

To proclaim the Gospel, encourage spiritual growth through Scripture and prayer, and serve our neighbours with compassion.

Our vision

A community strengthened by hope.

To see people and communities strengthened by the hope of Christ and equipped to live out their faith in everyday life.

Our goal

Connecting people with God and one another.

To make room for worship, belonging and service — so that no one has to walk through life alone.

What matters to us

Faith lived together.

Four commitments shape the rhythm of our life as a church.

01

We gather to honour God and make room for prayer, praise and gratitude.

The journey so far

Every chapter has been grace.

We do not have a straight line to show you. We have a faithful God and a family that keeps saying yes.
