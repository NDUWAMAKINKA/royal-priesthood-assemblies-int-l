Ministries & fellowships

A place to belong and to grow.

Church is more than a Sunday service. It is men and women, youth and children, homes and neighbourhoods — learning to follow Jesus together.

Find your placePlan a visit

Life together

Every season has a room.

We want every person who walks through our doors to find more than a seat — a place to grow, to serve and to be known.

Our ministries are not separate churches. They are expressions of one family, each making room for a different season of life while keeping the same centre: the Word, prayer and love.

“And let us consider one another in order to stir up love and good works, not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together.”

Hebrews 10:24–25

Our ministries

Find your people.

Four communities, one family. Each one is a doorway into deeper fellowship and service.

Men

Royal Men’s Fellowship (The Pillars)

A brotherhood of men learning to lead with integrity, pray with courage and carry responsibility with grace. We sharpen one another, study the Word and serve the house together.

Ask about the fellowship
Women

Virtuous Women’s Ministry

A community of women growing in faith, purpose and friendship. We pray together, study the Word, celebrate milestones and support one another through every season.

Ask about the ministry
Next generation

NextGen Youth & Teen Ministry

A place for young people to ask honest questions, grow in their own faith and discover their gifts. We combine fun, friendship and discipleship that meets them where they are.

Home cells

Home Cell & Branch Fellowships

Church beyond Sunday. Home cells gather across Lusaka for prayer, Bible study, encouragement and practical support. It is where the family becomes personal.
