Questions

Before you join

Do I need a Jitsi account or an app?

No. The room opens in your browser. Allow the camera and microphone when the browser asks. No account is needed.

How long can a meeting last?

Jitsi’s free public service currently limits a meeting to 95 minutes. For a longer service, the church uses the backup FreeConferenceCall link shown on the meeting card.

Can I join by phone instead of video?

If the pastor has published a backup dial-in number and access code, they appear on the meeting card above. If they are not shown, use the room on a phone or computer.

What is the backup meeting link?

Jitsi is the main room. If the church has set a backup FreeConferenceCall link, it appears on the meeting card for large services or if Jitsi is unavailable.

I did not receive the email. What should I do?

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it is still missing, request the link again, or contact the church office and someone will help.

Who do I contact for help?

Email royalpriesthoodassembliesintl@gmail.com or message the church on WhatsApp. The contact buttons are below.
