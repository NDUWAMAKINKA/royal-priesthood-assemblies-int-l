A few helpful answers

Before you come.

What should I expect?

A warm welcome, worship, prayer and a practical message from the Word. You are free to participate as much as you are comfortable.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some people dress up, others come in everyday clothes. What matters is that you are here.

Is there something for children?

Yes. Children are part of the family. Ask our welcome team about children’s activities and NextGen on the day.

Can I share my card on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the Share button on a supported phone, or tap Download card and attach the image to any message.
