Before you come

A few practical answers.

Do I need to tell you I am coming?

You are always welcome to simply arrive. If you use the visit form, we can look out for you and help with transport or directions.

Is there parking?

Yes. Otwa Events has parking at the venue. Our welcome team will help you find your way.

What time should I arrive?

Arrive a little before 09:00 AM so you have time to park, meet the welcome team and settle in.

Can I bring my children?

Yes. Children are part of the family. Ask our team about children’s activities and NextGen on the day.

How do I ask about transport?

Send a WhatsApp message or use the visit form above. We will confirm current availability and pickup points.
