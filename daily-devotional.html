Daily devotional

Start today with God.

A quiet moment in the Word can change the shape of your day. Read, reflect and pray — then carry His presence with you.

Read today’s devotionalWatch a message

Today’s word

“Through the Lord’s mercies we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not. They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness.”

Lamentations 3:22–23

God’s mercy is not a reserve for special days. It is new this morning — for the meeting you are dreading, the conversation you need to have, the strength you feel you do not have.

Begin here: receive His mercy, then walk in it. You do not have to manufacture faithfulness today; you are invited to trust the One who is faithful.

A simple rhythm

Read. Reflect. Pray.

  • ReadOpen the Word and read slowly. Ask the Holy Spirit to speak to you.
  • ReflectWhat does this reveal about God? What is He asking of you today?
  • PrayTurn what you have read into conversation — thanks, confession, request, surrender.
  • CarryChoose one small act of obedience and do it today.
