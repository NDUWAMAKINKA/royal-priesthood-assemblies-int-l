Our Senior Pastor

A shepherd’s heart. A father’s faith.

Apostle Dr. Mighty Zunga leads Royal Priesthood Assemblies International with a clear commitment to the Word, prayer and the care of people.

Founder & General Overseer

A life given to the Word and to people.

Apostle Dr. Mighty Zunga is the Founder and General Overseer of Royal Priesthood Assemblies International in Lusaka, Zambia.

His ministry is expressed through preaching, prayer, discipleship and the leadership of the church community. He encourages believers to grow in their understanding of Scripture and to put their faith into practice through service.

Those who sit under his teaching describe a pastor who loves the Word, values prayer and believes that every person matters. His burden is to see a church family that is strong in faith, warm in fellowship and generous in love.

The call

A ministry shaped by prayer.

Before it was a public ministry, it was a private yes.

His ministry is expressed through preaching, prayer, discipleship, and the leadership of the church community. He encourages believers to grow in their understanding of Scripture and to put their faith into practice through service.

At the heart of his leadership is a simple conviction: the Word of God is enough to change a life, and prayer is the atmosphere in which that change takes root.

“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.”

Matthew 5:16

What he has given his life to

Four things that never move.

The priorities that shape his leadership and our life together.

01

The Word

Clear, faithful teaching that helps people know Jesus and live what they believe.

02

Prayer

Making room for God to speak, heal and guide — personally and as a church.

03

People

Pastoring the family, celebrating milestones and standing with people in hard seasons.

04

Raising leaders

Helping sons and daughters grow into their calling and take their place in the house.

He and the church

Leading a family, not just a service.

Apostle Zunga leads alongside the RPAI leadership team and the wider church family. Together they carry the work of the ministry — teaching, prayer, home cells, youth, women, men and practical care.

His heart is for a church where the next generation is not an afterthought, where the elderly are honoured, and where every person is seen and known.
