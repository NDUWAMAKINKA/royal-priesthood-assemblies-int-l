About us

A place of love where everyone counts

Royal Priesthood Assemblies International is a church family in Lusaka, Zambia. We gather to worship, grow in faith, and serve our community.

Know more about us

Love that forms disciples

In Royal Priesthood, love is more than a kind word; it is the life of Christ made visible in the way we welcome, forgive, serve, and stand beside one another. We are called to carry each other's burdens and to make room for every person to grow in faith.

As a royal priesthood, we worship God with our whole lives. As disciples, we follow Jesus and help others follow Him, sharing His truth with humility and living it with compassion at home, in the church, and throughout our community.

Scripture: John 13:34-35.
