Questions people ask

Before you come.

A few honest answers for first-time guests and friends.

What should I expect on Sunday?

A warm welcome, heartfelt worship, prayer and a practical message from the Word. The service usually lasts around two hours. You are free to participate as much as you are comfortable.

Where do you meet?

We meet at Otwa Events, Long Acres, along Ndeke Road in Lusaka. Sunday Divine Service begins at 09:00 AM. Midweek fellowship is on Wednesday at 05:30 PM.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some people dress up, others come in everyday clothes. What matters is that you are here.

Is there something for children and teenagers?

Yes. Our NextGen Youth & Teen Ministry and children’s activities make room for the next generation to learn, ask questions and belong.

How do I get involved?

Start by joining a Sunday service or a home cell. Then talk to us about the Royal Men’s Fellowship, Virtuous Women’s Ministry, NextGen or serving on a team. We will help you find your place.

Can I watch online?

Yes. You can join our video conference from your phone, tablet or computer, or follow the ministry on Facebook and YouTube.
