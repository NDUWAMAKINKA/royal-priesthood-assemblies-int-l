Give

Worship through generosity.

Giving is an act of worship. It supports the ministry of the Word, prayer, discipleship and practical care for people in our church family and community.

Talk to the church officeWhy we give
A word for the giver

“Honour the Lord with your possessions, and with the firstfruits of all your increase.”

Proverbs 3:9

Why we give

Generosity builds the family.

Every gift is an act of worship and a partnership in the work of the Gospel.

Your giving helps make room for teaching, prayer, discipleship and practical care. It supports the everyday work of the church — from the Sunday gathering to home cells and community outreach.

Thank you for partnering with what God is doing at Royal Priesthood Assemblies International. Please give prayerfully, cheerfully and according to what you have.

Ways to give

Give simply and securely.

Giving details are being updated. Please contact the church office for the current active account or mobile-money details before sending a gift.

Mobile money

Airtel · MTN · Zamtel

Mobile-money giving is available through the church’s current merchant details. Message the office and we will send the active number.

Ask for mobile-money details
Zambian banks

Bank transfer

Give directly to the church’s Zambian bank account. Account name: Royal Priesthood Assemblies International. Banks include Zanaco, Stanbic, Absa, FNB and Standard Chartered. Please contact the office for the current account number and branch.

International giving: ask the office for the current PayPal details before sending a gift.
