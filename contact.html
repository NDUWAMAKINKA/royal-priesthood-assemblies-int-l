Questions people ask

Before you reach out.

What is the best way to contact the church?

WhatsApp is usually the fastest. You can also call, email or send a prayer request through the form on this page.

How quickly will someone respond?

We aim to respond within a day or two. Prayer requests are handled by the pastoral team as soon as possible.

Can I request a visit from the pastor?

Yes. Send a message with your name, location and the reason for the visit. The office will follow up.

I am new. What should I do first?

Fill in the connect card or simply come to a Sunday service. Our welcome team will help you take the next step.
